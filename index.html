Treasure Hunt Official Rule Book

Strict guidelines governing fair play, navigation, and timing

The Treasure Hunt is a team-based challenge involving: QR codes, riddles, logic, observation, and teamwork.

The basic flow is:
Scan QR → Solve Riddle → Find Location → Scan Next QR → Repeat

The final clue leads the team back to the Starting Point. The winner is the team that successfully completes the route according to the rules and reaches the Starting Point first.

  • Every team must register before the event.
  • Every team needs a unique team name/number. Our team name: Existential Error.
  • Team members must stay together at all times.
  • Members cannot split up to search different locations simultaneously.
  • Only registered members can participate.
  • Note: Team size is not set here because the official rulebook leaves the exact number blank.
1. START

Teams begin together at the designated starting point.

2. SCAN

Scan the QR code to receive your assigned riddle clue.

3. SOLVE

Solve the riddle and identify the physical location.

4. FIND

Travel together as a single unit to that location.

5. SCAN AGAIN

Find and scan the next QR code in the specified sequence.

6. FINAL CLUE

Follow the final clue back to the starting point and check in with organizers.

To qualify as a winner, the team must:

  • Solve all required clues
  • Visit all required locations
  • Scan all required QR codes
  • Adhere strictly to every fair-play rule
  • Return successfully to the Starting Point together
⏱️ The timer starts when organizers announce “START!” and stops when the complete team reaches the Starting Point and checks in.
  • Scan QR codes in the correct, assigned sequence.
  • Do not scan QR codes belonging to other teams or parallel routes.
  • Do not move, hide, damage, cover, replace, or alter QR codes under any circumstances.
  • Do not photograph and share QR codes with other teams.
  • Report any damaged or unreadable QR codes immediately to organizers.
  • Do not bypass or manipulate the digital QR system.
  • Visit only the physical location indicated by the clue.
  • All team members must arrive at the location together.
  • Stay strictly within permitted campus areas.
  • Do not enter restricted or off-limit areas.
  • Do not climb walls, buildings, trees, gates, or structures.
  • Do not disturb classrooms, offices, laboratory equipment, or campus property.
  • Do not interfere with other participants.
  • Respect students, faculty, staff, visitors, and organizers at all times.
Phones ARE allowed for:
  • QR scanning
  • Reading clues and riddles
  • Solving riddles within team discussion
  • Maps / navigation if organizers explicitly permit it
  • Taking team notes
Strictly PROHIBITED on phones:
  • Searching the exact riddle text on Google (unless explicitly permitted).
  • Asking ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, or any AI tools to solve clues (unless explicitly permitted).
  • Calling or texting people outside the team for answers.
  • Sharing clues, codes, or solutions with other teams.

The rulebook allows teams to request official hints when stuck.

“Hint count and time penalties will be announced by the organizers.”

⚠️ Important: Once a hint is requested, it cannot be cancelled or refunded under any circumstance.

❌ Cheating or unauthorized assistance
❌ QR code tampering or relocation
❌ Splitting the team
❌ Sharing information across teams
❌ Following another team intentionally
❌ Entering restricted/danger zones
❌ Damaging campus property
❌ Violence, pushing or misconduct
❌ Shortcutting the hunt sequence
“No treasure is worth risking your safety.”
  • Walk carefully throughout the campus; do not run blindly.
  • Always follow official campus security instructions.
  • Use designated pedestrian paths and walkways.
  • Watch out for vehicles and traffic near parking/gates.
  • Avoid dangerous, construction, or restricted areas.
  • Do not run on staircases — step deliberately.
  • Do not push or physically interfere with other participants.
  • Report injuries or emergencies immediately to the nearest volunteer.
Final Time = Actual Completion Time + Penalty Time

Official Rulebook Example: If your team reaches the finish in 42 minutes but incurred 7 minutes of hint/infraction penalties, your official score is 49 minutes.

The team with the lowest valid final time wins.

Tie-Breakers: In case of identical final times, ties are broken by: 1) Lowest total penalty minutes incurred; 2) Clue verification check accuracy.

The whole team must reach the Starting Point together, report immediately to organizers, have route and QR verification checked, and have their official finishing timestamp recorded.

Organizers have full discretion to:

  • Give or deny hints
  • Apply official time penalties
  • Stop a team for rules evaluation
  • Disqualify teams for unfair conduct
  • Modify a route if an unexpected campus blockage occurs
  • Replace damaged or unreadable QR codes
  • Halt or pause the hunt in an emergency

Organizer decisions are final in all disputes.

Treasure Hunt Tips

Practical tactical guide for solving riddles, pacing, and seamless team coordination

🧠 Solve Smart

Read all four lines carefully before making a hasty guess. Early lines set the mood, but the 3rd or 4th line usually gives away the exact distinguishing campus element.

👀 Observe

Think about physical campus characteristics: What does one hear (sound of keyboard, silence), smell (chemicals, food), or touch (cold railings, keys, water)?

🗣️ Discuss

Let everyone pitch interpretations in the first 20 seconds. If two members strongly agree on a logical clue, cross-verify before sprinting.

🗺️ Navigate

Once you identify a location, move together as a single pack. Moving scattered invites disqualification and confuses communication.

📱 Phone Discipline

Use the phone strictly for scanning and reading riddles. Searching exact riddle text online or prompting AI models like ChatGPT/Gemini is strictly prohibited by official rules.

⏱️ Don't Get Stuck

If discussion reaches a dead end after several minutes, evaluate whether taking an official penalty hint is smarter than burning valuable clock time.

👥 Stay Together

Never split the team to cover multiple campus wings. If an organizer spots team members separated, your team faces instant penalties or disqualification.

🚫 Don't Follow Other Teams

Tailgating another team to find locations is prohibited and foolish: other teams may be on different clue sequences or heading down the wrong corridor.

🏃 Safety First

Speed is completely useless if you create a safety hazard or injure someone. Walk swiftly, keep eyes open near stairs and blind corners, and never sprint blindly inside academic blocks.

4-Member Squad Architecture

TEAM EXISTENTIAL ERROR — ROLE SUGGESTION

🧠 Riddle Analyst

Focuses on linguistic nuances, Devanagari metaphors, double meanings, and distinguishing hints in the poem.

🗺️ Navigator

Visualizes campus geography, stairs, wings, shortcuts, and maps the shortest safe path between buildings.

👀 Observer

Scans target locations upon arrival (door frames, notice edges, pillars, desks) to spot the hidden QR quickly.

📱 QR & Time Lead

Keeps scanner ready, reads clue aloud clearly, logs team split-times, and watches the clock continuously.

💡 Everyone should discuss the clue. Roles are for focus, not for preventing other members from participating.
